04 Project overview (1000 chars max)
-------------------------------------
HORKIOS is a Web3 escrow SaaS built on GenLayer Intelligent Contracts. It turns a working campaign agreement into a public, wallet-signed oath:

1. A creator defines weighted demands (e.g. "post must mention gm"), funds the escrow in native GEN, and shares a private invitation link.
2. The KOL reviews each demand, accepts or proposes later deadlines, and signs.
3. The KOL submits canonical X/Twitter post URLs as evidence.
4. GenLayer validators independently render the page, run an LLM, reach consensus, and the escrow auto-settles each milestone.

Key differentiators:
- No admin wallet, no operator override — neither party can unilaterally withdraw after activation.
- 1% platform fee charged only on successful KOL payouts. Refunds carry no fee.
- Everything is public: terms, wallets, evidence, and decisions are permanent on-chain.
- Verification uses gl.eq_principle.prompt_non_comparative for consensus across validators, not a single oracle.

Built with Next.js 16, React 19, TypeScript, genlayer-js, and a 761-line GenLayer Intelligent Contract with 11 write methods, 8 views, and full financial accounting.

06 How-to steps
---------------
Step 1: Connect a wallet on horkios.vercel.app
Step 2: Click "Create oath"
Step 3: Write the oath title, the X handle you expect the post from, and the amount of GEN to lock in escrow
Step 4: Write your demands — what you want the post to mention, who to tag, etc. Add as many demands as you like and assign GEN to each. Click "Fund and create oath"
Step 5: Copy the invitation link and send it to the KOL
Step 6: KOL opens the link, reviews demands, connects wallet, and accepts
Step 7: KOL makes the X/Twitter post, submits it on the dapp, clicks "Ask GenLayer to verify"
Step 8: If the post satisfies the demand, escrow pays the KOL wallet automatically
